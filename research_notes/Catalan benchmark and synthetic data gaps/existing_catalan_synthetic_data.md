# Existing Catalan Synthetic / Instruction / LLM-Generated Datasets and Pipelines (as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-11. About 18 tool calls. Primary sources are mostly Hugging Face dataset and model cards, plus the HF API listings for the projecte-aina and BSC-LT orgs. "Verified" means I read it on the primary card or API. "Inferred" is marked as such.

## Q1: What instruction/synthetic datasets did BSC / Projecte AINA release for Catalan (Salamandra / ALIA)?

### Takeaway
BSC/AINA is by far the largest producer. Its newest and biggest release is the **ALIA-2606 post-training suite** (SFT about 714k conversations, 87k of them Catalan; DPO helpfulness and safety sets; function-calling SFT). It was published on HF around June–July 2026, and most of the synthetic part was generated with **DeepSeek-V3-0324**. Older Catalan sets are InstruCAT (template-converted from task datasets), RAG_Multilingual (Mixtral 8x7B), dolly3k_ca and oasst1_ca (translations), mentor-ca (MT of MentorES), and mPersonas (DeepSeek-V3 personas, PersonaHub-style). I found no public generator code for any of these.

### Inferences
- The "aina instruct", "catalan instruct" and "CA-ES parallel" names in the brief map to InstruCAT, catallama/Catalan-Instruct (community, see Q2) and the projecte-aina/BSC-LT parallel corpora. I found no dataset literally named "MentorCA". It exists only as the `mentor-ca` subset inside the SFT mixes.
- BSC's pipeline is: DeepSeek-V3-0324 as generator and judge, persona seeding from FineWeb2, verifiable-constraint filtering, and translation of English reasoning data. This is the de facto state of the art for Catalan. A new generator should differentiate from it, for example with native-Catalan-first generation, open code, fine-grained linguistic quality control, or domain or grammar tasks, rather than reproduce general chat SFT.

### Gaps
- License and size of the DPO sets and of SFT-fc; contents of XitXatTools; how dolly3k_ca and oasst1_ca were translated (human vs MT). Not checked within the budget.
- I found no public repo with BSC's generation code (prompts or pipelines). Neither the cards I read nor the searches mentioned one, so it may be unreleased.

## Q2: Are there LLM-generated Catalan corpora (Cosmopedia/Magpie-style, community, Aya, SmolTalk, FineWeb-2)?

### Takeaway
Outside BSC, the main community effort is **CataLlama** (catallama org, 2024): Catalan-Instruct V1/V2, translated or ChatGPT-3.5-augmented, and Catalan-DPO V1/V2. I found **no Catalan Cosmopedia-style synthetic textbooks, no Catalan Magpie dataset, and no Catalan portion in the Aya dataset**. FineWeb-2 has a real (non-synthetic) Catalan web subset.

### Cited Findings
- **catallama/Catalan-Instruct-V2**: ~710k rows (696k train / 14.2k test), ~289M Llama-3 tokens, ~55% Catalan / 45% English, ">460k Catalan instructions". Built from InstruCAT (some categories augmented with ChatGPT-3.5) and RAG_Multilingual (fully rewritten with ChatGPT-3.5, Catalan only). About 250k instructions were translated into Catalan with an unnamed MT model. English data comes from orca-math, SlimOrca, ultrachat_200k, CodeFeedback and others. The license is inconsistent: cc-by-sa-4.0 in the metadata, CC BY 4.0 in the text. Circa mid-2024. V1 (catallama/Catalan-Instruct) also exists. — [catallama/Catalan-Instruct-V2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/catallama/Catalan-Instruct-V2)
- **catallama/Catalan-DPO-V2**: 23.5k preference pairs, ~70% more than V1. 9.5k pairs come from argilla/distilabel-intel-orca-dpo-pairs (50% translated into Catalan) and 7.2k from argilla/Capybara-Preferences-Filtered. The rest are synthetic pairs made by scoring CataLlama outputs with a reward model. — [Catalan-DPO-V2 commit](https://huggingface.co/datasets/catallama/Catalan-DPO-V2/commit/50170c71e74d7b033ac1f1e56c3f1d30dc6cdd7d) (from the search summary)
- **Aya dataset** (CohereLabs): ~204k human-written prompt/completion pairs, Apache 2.0. **Catalan is not among its languages.** — [CohereLabs/aya_dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/CohereLabs/aya_dataset)
- **FineWeb-2** `cat_Latn`: 15,512,049 documents, 16.66 GB. Real web text, not synthetic. Useful as seed or grounding documents, as BSC did for mPersonas. — [FineWeb-2 README](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-2/blob/cab26bb3cab1ca7c26bb13a48f80fc3d2def4620/README.md)
- Cosmopedia: 25B tokens of synthetic textbooks and stories from Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct, with public pipeline code. I found no Catalan variant. — [HF blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/cosmopedia), [GitHub](https://github.com/huggingface/cosmopedia)
- Magpie supports multilingual generation through system prompts (the paper demonstrates Chinese). I found no Catalan Magpie dataset. — [Magpie](https://digital.lib.washington.edu/researchworks/items/6d28bf73-93cf-4606-8f7a-5e010144453c)

### Inferences
- Catalan pretraining-scale synthetic text (textbooks, educational) appears not to exist publicly. That is an open niche, and FineWeb-2 Catalan could seed it.
- Catalan-Instruct V2 inherits InstruCAT's CC BY-NC-ND license, but its own card lists CC-BY/CC-BY-SA. Anyone reusing it should check this license conflict.

### Gaps
- I did not check SmolTalk2 or other multilingual SmolTalk variants, or Argilla community efforts, for Catalan coverage.
- I did not find a Catalan distilabel pipeline. The search returned nothing.

## Q3: Valencian-specific efforts (Aitana / GPLSI, etc.)

### Takeaway
GPLSI (Universitat d'Alacant) released the **Aitana** model family: Aitana-6.3B on FLOR-6.3B, and Aitana-2B-S / 7B-S continually pretrained from Salamandra on Valencian administrative, legal, parliamentary and tourism text. There are instruct variants, including tourism and "Aligned" versions. The models are tuned on "ALIA Instruction v12" plus GPLSI Valencian QA and instruction sets. GPLSI publishes mostly parallel and pretraining corpora. I found no standalone public Valencian synthetic instruction generator.

### Cited Findings
- Aitana-2B-S was continually pretrained from Salamandra-2B on Valencian data: 2 epochs, 2.12B tokens. Sources include DOGV, Les Corts Valencianes, the AMIC corpus, BOUA and tourism texts (dataset IDs like dogv_va_2025, amic_va_2025). — [Friendli mirror, Aitana-2B-S-base](https://friendli.ai/models/gplsi/Aitana-2B-S-base) (mirror of the HF card, not the primary source)
- Aitana-7B-S-Instruct was tuned on the "ALIA Instruction v12" dataset. Sources include LingComp QA, an instruct legal refugee dataset and an Amic parallel corpus. The card states it was NOT instruction-tuned on Catalan data. — [Friendli mirror, Aitana-7B-S-Instruct](https://friendli.ai/models/gplsi/Aitana-7B-S-Instruct)
- Aitana-2B-S-tourism-Instruct sources include CoQCat, SciFact, LingComp QA, TurismInstructionsGPLSI and DOGV Parallel. Aitana-2B-S-Instruct sources include OASST1/2 and M-Personas. — [Friendli mirror](https://friendli.ai/models/gplsi/Aitana-2B-S-tourism-Instruct), [Aitana-2B-S-Instruct](https://friendli.ai/models/gplsi/Aitana-2B-S-Instruct)
- Aitana-6.3B was trained on FLOR-6.3B with Valencian parliament transcripts (DSCCV). — [AlphaNeural mirror](https://app.alphaneural.io/assets/gplsi/Aitana-6.3B)
- GPLSI hosts Valencian–Spanish parallel corpora, such as gplsi/uji_parallel_va_es. BSC-LT added Spanish-Valencian_Catalan_Parallel_Corpus in Feb 2026. — [gplsi/uji_parallel_va_es](https://huggingface.co/datasets/gplsi/uji_parallel_va_es/tree/main/data/train); [HF API BSC-LT](https://huggingface.co/api/datasets?author=BSC-LT&limit=300)

### Inferences
- "ALIA Instruction v12" looks like an internal (pre-2606) version of the ALIA SFT mix that was shared with GPLSI. It does not appear as a public dataset under that name.
- A generator with a Valencian-variety option (AVL norms, different lexical and morphological choices) would fill a gap. Existing Valencian resources are domain corpora, not variety-controlled synthetic instructions.

### Gaps
- Aitana details come from third-party mirrors (Friendli, AlphaNeural), not the official HF gplsi cards. Those cards have typos (for example, a date range of "2001 to 1999"). I did not find CENID-specific datasets.
- I did not verify whether TurismInstructionsGPLSI is public, or how it was generated.

## Q4: Open-source synthetic data generators for Catalan or Romance languages; grammar and error-correction (GEC) generators

### Takeaway
I found **no open-source generator tool built specifically for Catalan**, and **no public Catalan GEC dataset or synthetic-error generator**. Softcatalà's corrector is rule-based (LanguageTool, more than 1,700 rules) and has no published error corpus that I could find. This is the clearest gap for the user's project.

### Cited Findings
- Softcatalà's Catalan LanguageTool checker has more than 1,700 rules (spelling, grammar, typography, style). A bot used it to fix more than 200,000 errors in the Catalan Wikipedia. — [UB Retrucs](https://www.ub.edu/retrucs/?p=5140)
- Searches for Catalan GEC corpora or synthetic GEC data returned only English, Korean, Basque and Spanish work. The Basque example used rule-based corruption of correct news text plus a Transformer and reached F0.5 0.87. That is a direct template for Catalan. — [search results incl. UPF repository](https://repositori.upf.edu/items/b738b9ac-deba-4ae8-97d3-6bb4167952a9); generic methods: [C4_200M](https://www.research.google/blog/the-c4_200m-synthetic-dataset-for-grammatical-error-correction/), [Tagged corruption](https://paperswithcode.com/paper/synthetic-data-generation-for-grammatical)
- Language-agnostic pipelines exist that could be adapted: Cosmopedia (public code), Magpie, PersonaHub (as used by BSC's mPersonas). — sources in Q2

### Inferences
- LanguageTool's open Catalan rule files (each rule pairs an error pattern with a suggestion) could be inverted to inject realistic errors into clean FineWeb-2 Catalan, giving synthetic GEC pairs. This is a plausible, apparently unclaimed niche. It is not verified that no one has done it.
- The Spanish→Catalan code-switching synthesis used for ASR (translating noun chunks into Spanish) shows a ready technique for producing "Spanish interference" negative examples.

### Gaps
- I did not search GitHub directly (the budget ran out), so small personal repos may exist. I did not check Softcatalà's GitHub, which has MT and other models, for error-annotated data.

## Q5: Catalan preference / RLHF / DPO datasets; chat arena data

### Takeaway
Public Catalan preference data exists. **BSC-LT ALIA-2606-DPO-helpfulness** (7.5k Catalan prompts) and **-safety** (5.4k Catalan prompts) are LLM-judged with DeepSeek-V3. **catallama Catalan-DPO V1/V2** (23.5k pairs, partly translated, partly reward-model scored) is the community option. I found no Catalan chatbot arena or human-preference voting dataset.

### Cited Findings
- ALIA DPO: helpfulness 52,081 prompts (ca 7,535), safety 18,033 (ca 5,397), plus ~16% exact instruction following. Pairs built from DeepSeek-V3-0324 ratings. — [ALIA-40b-instruct-2606](https://huggingface.co/BSC-LT/ALIA-40b-instruct-2606)
- Catalan-DPO-V2: 23.5k pairs (see Q2). — [catallama](https://huggingface.co/datasets/catallama/Catalan-DPO-V2/commit/50170c71e74d7b033ac1f1e56c3f1d30dc6cdd7d)
- projecte-aina/hhh_alignment_ca is a Catalan version of the HHH alignment eval (an evaluation set, not training data). EQ-bench_ca and CaBBQ (bias) are BSC-LT evals. — [HF API](https://huggingface.co/api/datasets?author=projecte-aina&search=ca&limit=300)
- Searches for a Catalan LLM arena returned only the general LMSYS Chatbot Arena. — [Chatbot Arena paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.04132v1)

### Inferences
- All Catalan preference data is AI-judged. Human-annotated Catalan preferences are a gap.

### Gaps
- I did not verify whether LMArena exposes a Catalan language filter or Catalan vote counts.

## Q6: Reported quality issues with translated or synthetic Catalan data (translationese, Spanish interference)

### Takeaway
I found little direct published evidence on Catalan synthetic-data quality. The general literature shows LLMs produce more translationese in non-English languages. Catalan–Spanish interference shows up as grammatical shifts, spelling errors and lexical calques. Much of the existing Catalan SFT data is machine-translated (mentor-ca, openr1-math-translated, half of Catalan-DPO V2, ~250k in Catalan-Instruct V2), so the risk is real but not quantified for these datasets.

### Cited Findings
- A 2026 study finds LLM generations in non-English languages show substantial translationese; Spanish outputs averaged 45.7% classified as "translated". — [arXiv 2608.17399](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.17399) (from the search snippet)
- Catalan–Spanish translation errors are mostly grammatical shifts, spelling inaccuracies and lexical calques (human-translation study, a proxy only). — [Karazin periodical](https://periodicals.karazin.ua/philology/article/view/28130)
- BSC filters synthetic data with GlotLID language ID and MinHash/LaBSE dedup, removing 4–35% of generated content depending on language. That indicates a nontrivial share of off-language or duplicate output. — [m-personas](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BSC-LT/m-personas)
- Aitana instruct cards explicitly say they were not tuned on Catalan data despite targeting Valencian, which shows variety mismatch is a live concern. — [Friendli mirror](https://friendli.ai/models/gplsi/Aitana-7B-S-Instruct)

### Inferences
- Language-ID filters like GlotLID will not catch Spanish calques inside otherwise-Catalan text. A generator with a Catalan-specific interference or normativity checker (for example LanguageTool-ca rules as a filter, or a calque lexicon) would be a differentiator.

### Gaps
- I found no paper that measures translationese or castellanismes in Catalan instruction datasets specifically, and no Salamandra or ALIA report section on this. The Salamandra report (arXiv 2502.08489) was not read in full.
